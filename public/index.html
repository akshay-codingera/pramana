About the PRAMANA Legal Intelligence Platform

PRAMANA is an epistemological and computational research framework for predicting and explaining legal appeal dispositions in the Supreme Court of India. By modeling judicial documents hierarchically—first from tokens to 512-word semantic chunks via word-level bidirectional recurrent units with additive attention, and subsequently from chunks to full document representations—the architecture preserves long-range judicial argumentation while mitigating catastrophic forgetting across multi-page judgments.

Every prediction is explained via counterfactual Leave-One-Chunk-Out (LOCO) perturbation, isolating passages whose presence materially alters the predicted probability of the appeal being allowed or dismissed.

1. Hierarchical Neural Architecture (H-BiGRU + Additive Attention)

A legal judgment is segmented into non-overlapping or 50-token overlapping chunks $C = \{c_1, c_2, \dots, c_K\}$, where each chunk contains at most $L = 512$ word tokens.

Word Embedding: e_{i,j} = W_e \cdot w_{i,j}
Word-level BiGRU: h_{i,j} = [\overrightarrow{\text{GRU}}(e_{i,j}); \overleftarrow{\text{GRU}}(e_{i,j})]
Word Attention: u_{i,j} = \tanh(W_w h_{i,j} + b_w), \quad \alpha_{i,j} = \frac{\exp(u_{i,j}^\top u_w)}{\sum_k \exp(u_{i,k}^\top u_w)}
Chunk Vector: s_i = \sum_j \alpha_{i,j} h_{i,j}
Chunk-level BiGRU: v_i = [\overrightarrow{\text{GRU}}(s_i); \overleftarrow{\text{GRU}}(s_i)]
Chunk Attention: m_i = \tanh(W_c v_i + b_c), \quad \beta_i = \frac{\exp(m_i^\top u_c)}{\sum_k \exp(m_k^\top u_c)}
Document Representation: d = \sum_i \beta_i v_i
Classification: \hat{y} = \sigma(W_o d + b_o)

2. Leave-One-Chunk-Out (LOCO) Counterfactual Formulation

The marginal influence $\Delta P_i$ of chunk $c_i$ is computed as:

\Delta P_i = P(Y = \text{ALLOWED} \mid D) - P(Y = \text{ALLOWED} \mid D \setminus \{c_i\})
